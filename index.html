SVS Pulse — AI-Powered Delivery Intelligence In production

An AI-powered platform I started on my own and built from scratch. Delivery data was spread across hundreds of CXTM projects, so preparing one customer report meant opening dozens of tabs by hand. SVS Pulse brings every project into one view, and people simply ask it questions: Ask SVS Pulse answers in plain language ("which projects are trending down?", "prep my customer call"), and a Pulse MCP server brings the same answers into Claude and AI coding tools. Both answer from pre-structured records scoped to each user's access, instead of pulling raw data for every request. Runs in production with a staging environment; PRD approved by CX-PM, with Cisco IQ integration upcoming. I also led its booth at an internal Cisco showcase.
