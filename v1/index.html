How EcoVisionaries finds people worth meeting in Work on Climate, a Slack community of 40,000. It took first runner-up at the CX Tech4Social Good hackathon in April 2026.

First, read the ask.

Intent extraction turns a post in Slack into who someone is and who they want to meet.

Then imagine the match.

HyDE writes the profile of an ideal connection, which is far easier to search for than a vague request.

Search every source.

Member profiles, past posts and channel history, retrieved in parallel from PostgreSQL with pgvector.

Rank, then reply.

A model re-ranks the shortlist against the original intent before the bot answers in the thread.
